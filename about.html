Our Mission

Bridging the gap between mountains and opportunity

In the rugged terrain of the Himalayas, where a simple pencil is a precious commodity and children trek miles just to reach school, we stand as a beacon of hope and progress.

We're not just setting up computer labs and bringing Wi-Fi to villages — we're empowering children with essential 21st century life skills, preparing them for a future where they can thrive and contribute meaningfully to society.

Our efforts extend to supporting local schools initiated by the community itself, ensuring that every child has access to tools for success regardless of their geographical constraints. We're not just changing lives — we're shaping futures.

The Idea

Krishiv & Vansh — founders with a shared vision

Krishiv's Vision: During summers spent in his grandmother's remote village in Uttarakhand, Krishiv frequently witnessed the disparities between rural and urban education. He often observed children walking miles to schools with little infrastructure or technology, which deepened his desire to bring change. These experiences shaped his vision for Walk The Summit.

Vansh's Dedication: Inspired by Krishiv's stories and driven by a passion for change, Vansh co-founded Walk The Summit. Together, they are working to level the playing field for rural students, ensuring access to modern educational tools and opportunities.

From two high school best friends to a nationally recognised movement — their journey proves that the biggest mountains can be climbed one determined step at a time.
