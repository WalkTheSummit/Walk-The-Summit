CSR Initiative · Mussoorie, Uttarakhand

The ₹15 Lakh School Bus

Our most impactful project to date — facilitating a CSR partnership to provide a brand-new Force Trax mini school bus to a rural partner school. Children who once trekked 8-10km daily through dangerous mountain terrain now have safe, reliable, and dignified transport every single day.

Education & Outreach

CSR Initiatives That Inspire

Safe School Transport

In mountainous areas like Mussoorie, students often have to trek 8-10 kilometres daily on steep, uneven terrain through forests and rough paths — even during extreme weather. This leads to low attendance, student fatigue, and dropout risks, especially among younger children and girls. The newly provided Force Trax bus changes that reality completely.

Digital Classrooms

We set up fully functional computer labs in village schools that previously had no digital access. Each lab is equipped with computers, reliable internet via Wi-Fi, and a curriculum designed to build foundational digital literacy skills. We've reached 70+ rural classrooms to date.

Sports & Holistic Development

Education isn't just textbooks. We provide stationary kits and sports equipment to promote the holistic development of students in partner schools — fostering teamwork, discipline, and joy in learning through play.

Himalayan International School Partnership

Walk The Summit has joined hands with the Himalayan International School to continue the transformation of education in the region. This long-term partnership brings sustained resources, qualified educators, and structured curriculum to students who need it most. We need your support to expand further.
