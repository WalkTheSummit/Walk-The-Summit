Transparency

Where does the money go?

100% of donations go directly toward education programmes and infrastructure. We are fully transparent about how every rupee is used.

01 / Equipment

Digital Equipment

Computers, tablets, projectors and internet routers for rural classrooms that have never had digital access before.

02 / Logistics

Safe Transport

School buses and transport solutions so children don't have to trek dangerous mountain terrain to attend school.

03 / Personnel

Skilled Teachers

Connecting qualified educators with under-resourced mountain schools and funding structured curriculum programmes.
