Our Mission

Geography should never determine the quality of education

In the rugged terrain of the Himalayas, where a simple pencil is a precious commodity and children trek miles just to reach school, Walk The Summit stands as a beacon of hope and progress.

Founder's Story

Two friends. One shared purpose.

Krishiv spent summers in his grandmother's remote village in Uttarakhand, where he repeatedly witnessed children walking miles to schools with little infrastructure or technology. These experiences shaped his vision: every child deserves access to the opportunities they deserve.

Vansh, inspired by Krishiv's stories and driven by his own passion for change, co-founded Walk The Summit. Together, they are leveling the playing field for rural students — ensuring access to modern educational tools and a gateway to the world.

Their journey began in high school and has since grown into a nationally recognised movement touching thousands of lives across the Himalayan region.
