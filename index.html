Education & Outreach

CSR Initiatives That Inspire


At Walk The Summit, we focus on unlocking real opportunities for students in remote Himalayan regions. One of our most impactful collaborations yet has resulted in the successful provision of a brand-new Force Trax mini school bus, worth over ₹15 lakhs, to a rural partner school in Mussoorie.

This was made possible through a CSR partnership with a reputed multinational company, which we helped connect directly to the school.

In mountainous areas like Mussoorie, students often have to trek 8-10 kilometres daily on steep, uneven terrain through forests, rough paths and even during extreme weather. This leads to low attendance, student fatigue and dropout risks, especially among younger children and girls. The newly provided bus changes that reality. The Force Trax, known for its durability and strength in tough terrains, now ensures that children have safe, reliable and dignified transport every day. What was once a struggle is now a smoother ride toward education, confidence and consistency.

This achievement is a testament to the power of meaningful partnerships and youth-led action. We’re proud to have played a role in bridging the gap between communities in need and institutions willing to help.



About

Our Mission


At Walk The Summit, our mission is to bridge the educational divide between urban and rural areas in the Himalayan region. We aim to provide children in remote villages with the resources and skills they need to grow academically and personally. Education, for us, is not just about acquiring knowledge, it’s about building confidence, fostering creativity, and empowering students to excel in today’s world.

Our approach blends traditional learning with modern technologies, ensuring students can connect with their cultural roots while exploring the global community. By focusing on critical thinking, problem-solving, and independence, we aim to break generational cycles of inequality and unlock the potential of every child. Education is our way to create a sustainable, fair, and opportunity-filled future.

“To show them that their dreams can soar as high as the peaks that surround them."

With every project, we strive to meet the needs of today while preparing for the challenges of tomorrow. Our impact is both immediate and long-term, ensuring the children and communities we serve can thrive for generations to come.

Walk The Summit Honored with the Prestigious PHD Chamber of Commerce and Industry Junior Astitva Samman Award

We are thrilled to share that Walk The Summit (WTS) has been honored with the PHDCCI Junior Astitva Samman Award by the PHD Chamber of Commerce and Industry. This prestigious recognition celebrates our commitment to transforming education in the rural Himalayas and empowering underprivileged students.

The award was presented by Lt. Gen. Vinod Khandare (Principal Advisor, Ministry of Defence) and Smt. Savitri Thakur (State Minister, Women & Child Development), marking a significant milestone in our journey. This achievement reinforces the impact of youth-led initiatives and motivates us to continue striving for meaningful change in underserved communities.

We extend our heartfelt gratitude to PHDCCI for this honor and to everyone who has supported our mission. This award is not just a recognition but a reminder that every effort towards educational equity brings us closer to a brighter future for all.

Empowering Gaddi Tribe Students with 21st-Century Skills

Walk The Summit has been working closely with students from the Gaddi tribe in Dharamshala, a nomadic pastoralist community with limited access to modern education. Through this initiative, we have introduced 21st-century skills such as public speaking, leadership, critical thinking, and digital literacy, equipping students with the tools they need to thrive in a rapidly changing world.

Shaping a Future of Opportunity

For many Gaddi tribe children, learning was once confined to textbooks and traditional methods. By introducing new ways of thinking and engaging with the world, we are helping them develop the confidence and skills needed to navigate their futures with curiosity, resilience, and ambition.

Empowering Education at Himalayan International School

At Walk The Summit, we believe that education should be engaging, accessible, and future-ready. Our work at Himalayan International School near Kempty Falls, Mussoorie, reflects this commitment, bringing technology, resources, and essential skills to students who need them the most.
